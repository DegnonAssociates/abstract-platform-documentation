This form will be created in order to capture relevant reviewer information and intent to review submissions. Unlike previous years, the reviewer accounts will also be linked to Neon, so information like name, email, institution, and other similar personal information will already be stored in Neon and will not be required to ask on this form. 

Information such as topics they wish to review, submission types willing to review, and other abstract-system specific questions should be asked here.

The form can be shown on the submission system by going to [[General Configuration#Add call for reviewers|General Configuration -> Add Call for Reviewers]] and checking the checkbox. This will allow users who sign in to register to be a reviewer.
